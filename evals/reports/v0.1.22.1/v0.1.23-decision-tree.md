**H1: Validator-too-strict (vs eval-metric mismatch)**

The production validator marks citations as invalid that the eval-metric would consider valid under the hierarchical containment rule. This suggests a mismatch between:
- Production-side citation validation (H4 STRICT byte-unchanged since v0.1.18)
- Eval-side citation matching (hierarchical containment rule introduced in v0.1.18 ADR-0024)

Cases where Auditor escalates to RHR may be over-conservative.
