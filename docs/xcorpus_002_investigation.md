## For TFM memoria — diagnosed-and-attempted, deeper-ceiling confirmed (WHAT/WHY/HOW/IMPACT)

This section is the memoria-ready synthesis (per the `feedback_optimization_narrative_doc` discipline). Both the v0.1.9 diagnostic and the v0.1.10 attempted-fix are memoria-worthy.

### v0.1.9 finding (diagnostic only)

- **WHAT** — diagnosed: standard `BAAI/bge-reranker-v2-m3` cross-encoder fails on cross-corpus multi-regulation queries (single-article dominance pattern).
- **WHY** — measured evidence (Calls 1+2+3): the reranker scores 5 paragraphs of NIS2 art 23 higher than any chunk of NIS2 art 35 or GDPR art 33; lowering `purity_threshold` 0.6→0.5 produces an IDENTICAL emitted set; the dense pool DOES contain all 3 expected articles.
- **HOW** — three candidate fixes identified: (A) per-article cap; (B) MMR; (C) hybrid score. Option A surgical → tackled in v0.1.10.
- **IMPACT** — v0.1.9 ships diagnostic + slow regression test only; NO production change; 1/3 expected articles surfaced (NIS2 23) unchanged.

### v0.1.10 finding (cap shipped backward-compat; measurement shows cap alone insufficient)

- **WHAT** — implemented: per-article deduplication cap via `RetrievalConfig.max_chunks_per_article: int | None = None`. Default `None` = no cap = production-byte-identical to v0.1.9. When set to N, caps each `(norma, article)` key to N chunks in the reranked list BEFORE the purity gate.
- **WHY** — surgical follow-up to v0.1.9 finding. Hypothesis: limiting NIS2 art 23 to ≤2 chunks frees slots for NIS2 art 35 and GDPR art 33. Pure deterministic helper; $0; backward-compat default.
- **HOW** — `_apply_per_article_dedup(ranked_triples, max_per_article)` operates on `(norma, article, payload)` best-first triples, preserves order, drops chunks beyond the cap for each `(norma, article)` key. Wired into `run_auto` BEFORE `_apply_purity_gate` when `cfg.max_chunks_per_article is not None`; otherwise the construction collapses to the exact v0.1.9 expression.
- **IMPACT** — measured (Call 4 + Call 5 in the table above):
  - Algorithm-level success: Call 4 emits 4 distinct NIS2 articles (`nis2.23, nis2.23, nis2.30, nis2.13, nis2.10`) instead of the v0.1.9 baseline `5×nis2.23`. The cap demonstrably recovers article diversity within a norma.
  - **System-level failure to fix xcorpus-002**: top-5 deduped is still 5/5 NIS2 (just 4 distinct articles instead of 1) → purity gate still collapses to NIS2 alone → NIS2 art 35 / GDPR art 33 never surface. Even cap=2 + `purity_threshold=0.5` (Call 5) produces an IDENTICAL emitted set to Call 4 because the dominant-norma share is 1.0 in both. **1/3 expected articles surfaced — unchanged from v0.1.9 baseline.** Production defaults unchanged (cap=None).
  - **The reranker bias is at NORMA level, not just ARTICLE level** for this query — a deeper structural finding than v0.1.9 anticipated.

### Three NEW fix candidates surfaced by v0.1.10 measurement

- **(i) Per-NORMA cap** — analogous to the per-article cap but at norma granularity. E.g. `max_chunks_per_norma=3` would force at least 2 of top-5 from a non-dominant norma. Architecturally identical to `_apply_per_article_dedup`; trivial ceremony, $0. Most surgical next step.
- **(ii) Raise `top_k` 5→12** — gives the gate more candidates; NIS2 art 35 / GDPR art 33 might appear in positions 6-12 of the deduped list (they're in the dense pool). Drawback: increases Analyst context size + chunk-budget assumptions downstream. Trivial ceremony, $0.
- **(iii) Different reranker model** — bge-reranker-v2-m3 may be unsuitable for cross-corpus questions; try a multilingual cross-encoder with different training data. Large ceremony, $0 local but requires full paid re-baseline.

Per the maximalist plan, the user authorizes whichever (i)/(ii)/(iii) lands next; v0.1.11 onward shifts accordingly. Single paid validation deferred to the post-microhito-bundle slot.
